Privacy policy

IIT Patna Student Alumni Association · Last updated 10 November 2026

What this site is

This site handles requests to join the association’s WhatsApp community. Existing members vouch for new members, so that only genuine students and alumni get in.

What we collect

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name and email address. We use them only to recognise you when you come back. We don’t access your Gmail, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.
  • What you enter: your full name, college roll number, branch or course, batch, and WhatsApp number.
  • What you do here: who you vouch for, and any objections you raise, including the reason you give.

Who can see it

  • Members can see a waiting applicant’s name, branch, batch and the last 4 digits of their WhatsApp number, so they can recognise people they know.
  • Admins can see everything above, including roll numbers, full WhatsApp numbers, who vouched for whom, and who objected and why. Admins use this to approve join requests in WhatsApp and to remove fake accounts.
  • If you object to someone, they can’t see your name or your reason.

Why we use it

Only to check that people joining the community are who they say they are, to match WhatsApp join requests to approved people, and to remove fake accounts along with the people who vouched them in.

Where it’s stored

Your information is stored in a database run by Supabase, hosted in Mumbai, India. Sign-in is handled by Google. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell or share your information with anyone, we don’t show ads, and we don’t use analytics or tracking.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your request is open or while you’re a member, plus the record of who vouched for whom, which protects the community. You can ask an admin to delete your information at any time.

Contact

For questions, or to have your information deleted, message any admin in the WhatsApp community.
